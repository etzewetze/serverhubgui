# Title banner

Original transparent parchment illustration created for ServerHub 2.5.1 with the built-in image generator. The linked stock preview was unavailable, so this is a new illustration rather than a copy of that stock image.

Prompt: One original horizontal vintage parchment ribbon banner for a Minecraft menu, transparent background, no text. Light cream writing panel with gentle bowed paper edges, curled and folded parchment ends, restrained brown outlines, no wooden poles. Uniform stretchable middle and decorative outer quarters. Cream #F1D9A4, parchment #DBB57C, fold #B78045, outline #644024. No watermark, scenery, signature or extra objects.

`tools/build_pack.py` compiles the source into 32-pixel title sprites with fixed left/right decorations and a stretchable middle. The title text is added at runtime.
